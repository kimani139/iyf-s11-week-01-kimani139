# Notion — Draft by teammate

Notion is a flexible workspace for notes, docs, and collaborative project planning. This draft expands the Notion section with templates and collaboration tips.

## Templates Useful for Students
- Lecture Notes template: structure for capturing lecture summaries and questions.
- Task Board (Kanban): track assignments and deadlines among teammates.
- Reading List: keep track of articles and resources with tags and status.

## Collaboration Tips
- Use comments and mentions (@) to assign tasks and ask questions.
- Share pages with specific permissions (view/comment/edit) depending on need.
- Use databases to create linked views for tasks, readings, and project milestones.
