# Canva — Draft by teammate

Canva is an online design tool that helps students create posters, presentations, and visuals easily.

## Why Canva Helps Students
- Many free templates for presentations, posters, and social posts.
- Drag-and-drop editor: change images, text, and colors quickly.
- Export options: PNG, JPG, PDF for reports or slides.

## Quick Start
1. Sign up at https://www.canva.com/.
2. Select a template (Presentation or Poster) and customize the content.
3. Use the Elements tab to add icons, shapes, and illustrations.
4. Download the final design as PDF or PNG for submission.
